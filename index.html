本固件适用于 Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense（带官方摄像头扩展板）。请确认你的硬件型号再刷写。

This firmware is for the Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense (with official camera daughterboard). Verify your hardware before flashing.

2. 固件烧录2. Flashing

Pick Wireless or Wired version. Checking Erase device is recommended.

3. 使用 3. Usage

无线版：连接热点 Cymple_Face → 打开 192.168.4.1 配网；有线版：插 USB 打开 CympleApp 即可。

Wireless: join AP Cymple_Face → open 192.168.4.1 to set WiFi; Wired: plug USB & open CympleApp.
